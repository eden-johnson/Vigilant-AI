# Safety-First Automotive Design System (VIGILANT AI)

## Visual Identity
A high-contrast, automotive-grade design system engineered for glanceability, safety, and reduced eye strain during night driving.

---

## Color Palette

### Core Palette
- **Surface (Background):** `#131313` (Deep Charcoal/Black)
- **Primary (Optimal/Safe):** `#39FF14` (Neon Lime) - Used for active sensors, "I'm Awake" status, and optimal scores.
- **Warning (Moderate Risk):** `#FFB300` (Amber) - Used for low-level fatigue warnings and caution states.
- **Critical (High Risk):** `#FF3B30` (Pulse Red) - Reserved for fatigue detection alerts and emergency interventions.

### Neutral/Functional
- **Text (High Contrast):** `#FFFFFF` (White)
- **Text (Secondary):** `#A1A1AA` (Zinc-400)
- **Borders/Dividers:** `#27272A` (Zinc-800)
- **Card/Container Surface:** `#1C1C1E` (Zinc-900)

---

## Typography
- **Primary Font:** Inter
- **Headings:** Bold, uppercase, tracking-widest for technical precision.
- **Labels:** Semibold, condensed tracking for data-heavy dashboard views.

---

## Component Specifications

### 1. Sliders (Sensitivity/Threshold)
- **Track:** 6px height, Zinc-800 background.
- **Active Fill:** Neon Lime (`#39FF14`).
- **Thumb:** Large 20px circle for easy manipulation in high-vibration environments.
- **Labels:** 0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% markers positioned above the track.

### 2. Status Gauges
- **Circular Progress:** 12px stroke width.
- **Color Logic:** Green (0-30%), Amber (31-70%), Red (71-100%).

### 3. Navigation (Side Bar)
- **Background:** `#18181B` (Zinc-900).
- **Active State:** 4px Neon Lime left border, Zinc-800 background, Neon Lime icon/text.
- **Inactive State:** Zinc-500 icon/text.

### 4. Alert Overlays
- **Urgency Mode:** Full-screen dimming with a pulse-red border (`#FF3B30`).
- **Primary CTA:** Solid Neon Lime background with black text for maximum prominence.

---

## Spacing & Layout
- **Grid:** 12-column layout for dashboard views.
- **Inner Spacing:** Consistent 24px/32px padding for containers.
- **Roundness:** 4px (Minimal) to maintain a technical, "AI-processor" look.
